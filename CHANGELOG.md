# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-21

- Vitruvius's five column-spacing systems with linked front and plan drawings,
  fixed-front and fixed-diameter comparisons, and four/six/eight-column fronts.
- An explicit eustyle counterexample: equalize the central opening and measure
  the resulting change in diameter or front width.
- Seven Latin excerpts with new English, six Greek word-parts, and four complete
  source pages. Araeostyle gaps remain labeled as chosen illustrations.
- Shareable settings and self-contained HTML readings with exact embedded scans;
  both architecture workshops remain available offline.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

- Interactive Doric corner geometry: aligned centres, widened end metope and
  contracted end bay, with linked front elevation and plan.
- Measurements and comparison of the regularities each treatment preserves.
- Five architectural terms linked to Greek and Latin vocabulary.
- Vitruvius IV.3.2 in Latin with a new English translation and bundled scans.
- Shareable geometry, self-contained HTML studies and offline access.
