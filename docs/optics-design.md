The upper ray changes from longer to shorter. A separate, explicitly modern
control turns the face toward each new eye position. That contrast is the
experiment's central operation; the ancient text does not supply an observer
distance or a pinhole camera.

controls and units. The source photograph identifies an actual Ionic capital;
